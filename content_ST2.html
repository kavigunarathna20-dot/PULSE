SDG 3 • Good Health & Well-Being

Physical Activity and Exercise

Regular physical activity strengthens the body, improves mental well-being, and reduces the risk of many chronic diseases.

🏃

Active Lifestyle

Daily movement promotes overall physical fitness.

❤️

Heart Health

Exercise supports healthy cardiovascular function.

⚡

Energy & Fitness

Physical activity boosts stamina and energy levels.

Benefits of Exercise

  • Improved heart health
  • Stronger muscles and bones
  • Better mental well-being
  • Reduced risk of chronic disease
  • Increased energy levels

Common Barriers

  • Lack of time
  • Sedentary lifestyles
  • Poor motivation
  • Limited facilities
  • Excessive screen time

Encouraging Physical Activity

1

Education

Teach the importance of exercise.

2

Access

Provide safe spaces for recreation.

3

Participation

Encourage sports and active lifestyles.

4

Consistency

Make exercise a regular habit.

Physical Activity and SDG 3

Exercise promotes healthier lives and contributes directly to achieving SDG 3 through disease prevention and improved well-being.
