Good Health & Well-being

SDG 3 aims to ensure healthy lives and promote well-being for all at all ages. PULSE is dedicated to raising awareness and driving action for better health outcomes worldwide.

  • Regular exercise reduces risk of chronic disease by up to 50%
  • Mental health matters — 1 in 4 people will experience a mental health issue
  • Access to clean water and sanitation prevents 80% of disease

Why Health Matters

Mental Wellness

Mental health is fundamental to our collective and individual ability to think, emote, interact, earn a living, and enjoy life. Prioritizing mental wellness builds resilience.

Physical Vitality

Regular physical activity strengthens bones and muscles, improves cardiovascular health, and boosts the immune system. An active body fuels a vibrant life.

Community Care

Strong communities support health through shared resources, social connection, and collective action. When we care for each other, everyone thrives.
